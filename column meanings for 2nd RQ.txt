Codebook:

JAAR_VKL = year of record

'AP3_CODE'= Unique identification of the outcome of the traffic accident, divided into 3 categories;
"DOD",death
"LET", injury
"UMS"," Material damage


'WSE_ID', = Unique identification of the road situation at the location of the traffic accident; reference to reference file Road situations.
"WSE_ID","WSE_OMS"

Categories: straight road (1,6), intersection (4,5), 
1,"Rechte weg"-> “Straight road”
2,"Bocht" -> “Bend”
3,"Rotonde" -> “Roundabout”
4,"Kruispunt  3 takken" -> Intersection with 3 branches”
5,"Kruispunt  4 takken" -> “Intersection with 4 branches”
6,"Rechte weg  gescheiden rijbanen" -> “Straight road with separated lanes”
7,"Invoegstrook op (auto)snelweg" -> “On-ramp to (motor) way”
8,"Uitvoegstrook op (auto)snelweg" -> “Off-ramp from (motor) way”

'MAXSNELHD', =  The indicated maximum speed at the location of the traffic accident:
15 = 15 km/h (walking pace)
30 = 30 km/h
50 = 50 km/h
60 = 60 km/h
70 = 70 km/h
80 = 80 km/h
90 = 90 km/h
100 = 100 km/h
120 = 120 km/h
130 = 130 km/h

'WVG_ID', = Unique identification of the road surface at the location of the traffic accident;
1 - ZOAB, 2 - other asphalt, 3 - concrete, 4 - vowels, 5 - unpaved, 6 - gravel tiles, 7 - wood tiles

'OTE_ID', = Unique identification of the object type; reference to reference file Object types

OTE_OMS                         OTE_ID   

Motorcycle	                        31
Moped	                                    61
Light moped	                        62
Bicycle	                                    64
Mobility scooter	            65
E-bike	                                    66



LGD_ID
​​Unique identification of the road lighting at the location and time of the traffic accident
1,"Brandend" -> “Street light on”
2,"Niet brandend" -> “Street light not on”
3,"Niet aanwezig" -> “Street light absent”


Aol_id: Unique identification of the nature of the accident assigned to the traffic accident

"AOL_ID","AOL_OMS"
3,"Dier" -> “Animal”
9,"Eenzijdig" -> “Unilateral”
7,"Flank" -> “Flank”
6,"Frontaal" -> “Frontal”
2,"Geparkeerd voertuig" -> “Parked vehicle”
8,"Kop/staart" -> “Head/tail”
5,"Los voorwerp" -> “Loose object”
0,"Onbekend" -> “Unknown”
4,"Vast voorwerp" -> “Fixed object”
1,"Voetganger" -> “Pedestrian”


WDK_ID
Unique identification of the condition of the road surface at the location and time of the traffic accident

"WDK_ID","WDK_OMS"
1,"Dry"
2,"Wet"
3,"Snow/ice"
